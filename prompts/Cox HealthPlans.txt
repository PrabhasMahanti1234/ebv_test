🚨 JSON STRING ESCAPING RULES (CRITICAL FOR DEVICES)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When extracting drug names, especially for medical devices and supplies (pages 74-113):

1. **INCH SYMBOLS (") MUST BE ESCAPED AS \"**
   ❌ WRONG: "BD INSULIN SYRINGE 29G X 1/2" 1 ML"
   ✅ CORRECT: "BD INSULIN SYRINGE 29G X 1/2\" 1 ML"

2. **BACKSLASHES (\) MUST BE DOUBLE-ESCAPED AS \\**
   ❌ WRONG: "text\value"
   ✅ CORRECT: "text\\value"

3. **DOUBLE QUOTES IN TEXT MUST BE ESCAPED**
   ❌ WRONG: "GAUZE PAD 2"X2""
   ✅ CORRECT: "GAUZE PAD 2\"X2\""

4. **ALL OTHER CHARACTERS ARE PRESERVED EXACTLY**
   - Parentheses: (2 PEN), (GEN 5), (0.5 ML)
   - Percent: 70 %
   - Forward slashes: 1/2, 5/16, X
   - Hyphens and dashes: 29G X 1/2\", 150-21
   - Spaces: preserve exactly
